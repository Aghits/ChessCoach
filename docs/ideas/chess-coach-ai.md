# ChessPrinciple AI: Actionable Chess Insights for Club Players

## Problem Statement
How might we translate raw Stockfish engine evaluations into memorable, practical "pocket rules" and positional principles so that 1600–2100 Elo club players stop repeating the same strategic mistakes and develop reliable mental models for their future games?

---

## The Core Product Concept: The "Diagnosis + Pocket Rule" Format

Traditional chess engines tell players **what** happened (`-2.4 blunder: 18... Nxd4`). 
ChessPrinciple AI tells players **how to think** during the game by pairing every critical turning point with a memorable mental checkpoint.

### Example Explanations Generated by the Engine + LLM:

#### Case A: Tactical Blindspot & Coordination
* **Move:** `14... Na5?` (Engine Best: `14... Be6`, Eval: `+0.2` to `+2.8`)
* **Diagnosis:** "14... Na5 moves your knight away from the center and severs communication between your queen and the e7 bishop, enabling White's 15. Nd5 fork."
* **Actionable Pocket Rule:** **"Loose Pieces Drop Off (LPDO)."** Before redirecting a piece away from the defense of your center, audit all remaining minor pieces to ensure they are firmly anchored.

#### Case B: Inactive / Passive Play (Positional)
* **Move:** `21. a3?!` (Engine Best: `21. Rfe1`, Eval: `+1.4` to `+0.4`)
* **Diagnosis:** "21. a3 is a passive waiting move against a phantom threat. Black had no immediate attack, so White needed to seize the open e-file with 21. Rfe1."
* **Actionable Pocket Rule:** **"Activate the Lazy Piece."** When there is no concrete tactical fire to extinguish, do not push flank pawns—identify your least active piece and put it on an open file or outpost.

#### Case C: Pawn Structure & Bad Bishops
* **Move:** `18... c5?` (Engine Best: `18... Rad8`, Eval: `0.0` to `+1.9`)
* **Diagnosis:** "18... c5 permanently fixes your pawns on light squares (c5, d6, e5), turning your c8 bishop into a 'tall pawn' that will be locked out of the game."
* **Actionable Pocket Rule:** **"Opposite-Color Harmony."** Keep your pawns on the opposite color complex of your remaining bishop so your pieces retain free mobility.

## Technical Pipeline

```
Chess.com Game (PGN via public API)
        │
        ▼
1. Stockfish 16/17 WASM (Client-Side WebAssembly Worker)
   ├── Depth 16–18 calculation in browser (zero hosting cost)
   ├── Centipawn loss classification (Best, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Blunder)
   └── Top refutation line (SAN notation)
        │
        ▼
2. Deterministic Positional Extractor (chess.js)
   ├── Material balance & piece counts
   ├── Structural changes (isolated pawns, passed pawns, locked chains)
   ├── Loose/undefended pieces check
   └── Open files & outposts
        │
        ▼
3. Fact-Locked Prompt -> Free AI API (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash / Groq / OpenRouter Free)
   └── Returns Structured JSON:
       {
         "category": "Piece Coordination | Pawn Structure | Prophylaxis | King Safety",
         "diagnosis": "1-2 sentences on concrete tactical/structural consequence",
         "pocket_rule": "Punchy, memorable heuristic for future games",
         "refutation_preview": "1-2 engine moves demonstrating the refutation"
       }
        │
        ▼
4. Output Guardrail (Client-Side)
   └── Validates mentioned squares against legal board moves to guarantee 0% hallucination.
```

## Key Assumptions to Validate
- [x] **Zero Engine Cost:** Verified. Modern Stockfish 16/17 NNUE compiled to WebAssembly runs multithreaded inside client Web Workers with no server backend needed.
- [x] **Free AI API Accessibility:** Verified. Google AI Studio (Gemini 2.5 Flash) provides a free tier of 1,500 requests/day at 15 RPM. Groq and OpenRouter (`:free`) provide seamless OpenAI-compatible fallbacks.
- [ ] **Heuristic Quality:** Testing prompts with 1800+ club game positions to verify that generated "pocket rules" feel like genuine International Master advice rather than generic textbook quotes.

## MVP Scope
1. **Import & Player Fetch:**
   - Input a Chess.com username to load recent Blitz/Rapid games.
   - Or paste any PGN directly.
2. **Clean Interactive Board:**
   - Smooth piece movements, arrow indicators for engine best moves and blunders.
   - Full move list with standard notation and engine eval bar.
3. **Turn-by-Turn Engine Review:**
   - Client-side Stockfish scans the game and badges critical moments (Blunders, Mistakes, Missed Wins).
4. **Actionable Coaching Cards:**
   - Clicking a mistake displays the **Diagnosis** + **Actionable Pocket Rule** card.
   - "Try the Engine Move" button lets the user test the right continuation on the board.
5. **BYOK / Free Provider Settings:**
   - Clean settings modal to input a free Gemini or OpenRouter API key (stored securely in browser `localStorage`).

---

## Not Doing (and Why)
- **No Vague Engine Numbers Without Context:** We do not display raw `+3.14` numbers without explaining the human reason.
- **No Server-Side Engine Daemon:** Eliminates monthly cloud compute bills.
- **No Complex Social Layers:** No leaderboards, forums, or accounts required. Pure utility.
